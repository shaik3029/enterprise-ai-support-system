===============================================================================
ENTERPRISE GLOBAL SUPPORT & COMPLIANCE POLICIES (SLA & GOVERNANCE V4.2)
===============================================================================

1. SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT & BILLING CYCLES
   1.1 Invoicing and Payment Terms:
       All enterprise subscriptions are billed annually or monthly in advance. Payments 
       must settle within 30 calendar days from the invoice issuance date.
   1.2 Overdue Accounts and Service Suspension:
       If an account remains unpaid past 15 calendar days post-due date, system access 
       is transitioned to 'Read-Only'. Accounts overdue by 30+ days face automatic API 
       key revocation and temporary service suspension until all outstanding balances clear.
   1.3 Refund Eligibility & Cancellation Windows:
       - Enterprise Contracts: Non-refundable after the 14-day deployment onboarding window.
       - Pro/Standard Tiers: Eligible for a prorated refund if requested within 30 days of 
         the initial transaction. Processing requires 5 to 7 business days.

2. SECURITY, ACCESS CONTROL & ACCOUNT RECOVERY
   2.1 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Mandate:
       MFA is enforced across all enterprise admin accounts.
   2.2 Self-Service Identity & Password Reset:
       Users must initiate password resets via Settings > Security > Access Controls > Reset Credentials. 
       A Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) or SMS/Email verification token will be dispatched. 
       Tokens expire after 10 minutes.
   2.3 Regulatory & Transaction Notification Fees:
       Mandatory regulatory notification fees (e.g., statutory SMS transaction alerts, bank audit 
       logging charges such as INR 72/annum) are processed directly through local financial network partners 
       and reflected under 'Regulatory Overhead Fees'.

3. INCIDENT RESOLUTION & SLA TIER ESCALATION
   3.1 Severity Levels:
       - Sev-1 (Critical Outage): Initial response within 15 minutes; target mitigation within 2 hours.
       - Sev-2 (Major Feature Impairment): Response within 1 hour; resolution target within 8 hours.
       - Sev-3 (Minor Bugs / Guidance): Response within 24 hours.
   3.2 SLA Breach Compensation:
       If Sev-1 uptime drops below 99.9% in a given billing cycle, eligible enterprise clients receive 
       a 10% credit applied toward their next renewal invoice.

4. DATA RETENTION, PRIVACY & GDPR COMPLIANCE
   4.1 Data Encryption:
       All customer telemetry and vector embeddings are encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
   4.2 Right to be Forgotten (Data Erasure):
       Data deletion requests submitted to compliance@enterprise.ai will be executed within 30 days, 
       purging all indexed database records and vector stores.
